Our Story & Commitment to Puget Sound Homeowners

Founded in Seattle in 1998, AeroTherma began as a small family operation with a single service van and an unyielding commitment to doing things right. Over the past 28 years, as the HVAC industry has shifted toward high-pressure sales tactics and private equity buyouts, we have remained independent, local, and customer-focused.

Today, our team includes over 30 certified technicians, apprentices, and dispatch coordinators serving King, Snohomish, and Pierce counties. We invest in continuous technical education, equipping our team with factory certifications on modern cold-climate heat pumps, communicating inverter systems, and hospital-grade indoor air quality systems.
