Restore Peak Airflow and Stop Heating the Crawlspace

Your home’s ductwork functions as the respiratory system of your entire heating and air conditioning setup. When supply ducts are lined with decades of dust, pet dander, drywall dust from past remodels, or rodent droppings, every time your blower fan kicks on, those contaminants blow directly into your bedrooms and living spaces. Furthermore, the US Department of Energy estimates that the typical American home loses 20% to 30% of conditioned air through leaky duct seams, unsealed boots, and disconnected joints in attics or crawlspaces.

Our Thorough 4-Step Duct Cleaning Process

We do not use cheap shop vacuums or door-to-door carpet cleaning blowers. We follow strict NADCA (National Air Duct Cleaners Association) industry standards:

  1. Fiber-Optic Camera Inspection: We insert high-resolution borescope cameras into your supply and return lines to show you exactly what is hiding in your ducts before we begin.
  2. Negative Air Containment: We connect a commercial HEPA-filtered vacuum unit directly to your main trunk line. This creates strong negative pressure so no dust escapes into your home during cleaning.
  3. Rotary Brush Agitation: We feed flexible pneumatic whips and rotating brushes through every single register, scrubbing debris loose from galvanized metal and flexible duct walls.
  4. Sanitization & Final Verification: We apply an EPA-registered botanical antimicrobial treatment to neutralize odor-causing bacteria, followed by a final camera walk-through showing spotless ducts.

Frequently Asked Duct Questions

How often should home air ducts be cleaned?

The National Air Duct Cleaners Association recommends a professional cleaning every 3 to 5 years. However, you should schedule a cleaning sooner if you have completed home remodeling, adopted new shedding pets, moved into an existing home, or noticed excessive dust settling on furniture within 48 hours of cleaning.

Will duct cleaning make a mess inside my house?

Never. Because we place your entire ventilation system under deep negative pressure with commercial HEPA extraction units, all dislodged particles are pulled straight into our sealed containment system outside. Our technicians wear clean shoe covers and lay protective floor runners.

Is Aeroseal duct sealant safe for children and pets?

Yes. The Aeroseal sealant compound is a non-toxic, water-based vinyl acetate polymer—the same harmless material used in chewing gum and baby pacifiers. It emits zero hazardous VOCs and cures completely dry within two hours.
