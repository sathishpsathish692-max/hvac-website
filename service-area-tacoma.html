Tacoma HVAC Questions & Answers

Do I need a city permit to replace a furnace or heat pump in Tacoma?

Yes. The City of Tacoma requires mechanical and electrical permits for all heating and cooling replacements to verify electrical safety, proper circuit sizing, and ventilation codes. AeroTherma handles the entire permitting and inspection coordination process for you.

How quickly can you dispatch an emergency HVAC tech to Tacoma?

We maintain service trucks stationed throughout Pierce County. For no-heat emergencies during winter freezes, our typical on-site dispatch time is between 60 and 90 minutes. Call our 24/7 hotline directly at (800) 555-HVAC.

Do heat pumps work effectively in Tacoma during sub-freezing weather?

Absolutely. Modern cold-climate inverter heat pumps (such as Daikin Aurora and Mitsubishi Hyper-Heating) deliver 100% heating capacity down to 5°F and operate reliably down to -13°F without requiring costly electric resistance auxiliary heat.
