Common AC Problems We Fix Every Day

Most air conditioning breakdowns give subtle warning signs before failing completely. If you notice any of the following symptoms, our technicians can diagnose and fix the root cause before it leads to a costly compressor failure:

  • AC Blowing Warm or Ambient Air: Frequently caused by a blown dual-run capacitor, low refrigerant levels from a micro-leak, or a tripped outdoor breaker.
  • Ice Forming on Outdoor Unit or Copper Lines: Low refrigerant pressure or a severely clogged 1-inch air filter causes evaporator coils to freeze into a solid block of ice.
  • Water Leaking Near Indoor Furnace / Air Handler: Clogged condensate drain lines or a cracked overflow pan will trigger safety float switches or flood drywall.
  • Loud Humming, Clicking, or Screeching Sounds: Often indicates a worn fan motor bearing, failing contactor relay, or loose compressor mounting hardware.
  • Short-Cycling (System Runs 2 Minutes then Shuts Off): Oversized equipment, dirty condenser coils, or a malfunctioning thermostat sensor.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does an air conditioning repair typically cost?

Minor repairs such as clearing a clogged condensate drain or replacing a standard dual-run capacitor typically range between $140 and $280. More complex issues, like fan motor replacements or refrigerant leak repairs, average $300 to $650. We provide an exact upfront quote before any work starts.

How long does a new air conditioning installation take?

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day (usually 6 to 8 hours). Our crew arrives in the morning, installs and vacuums the refrigerant lines, tests the airflow, and hauls away your old equipment with zero mess left behind.
