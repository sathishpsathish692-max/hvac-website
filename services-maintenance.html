Why Regular HVAC Maintenance Is Essential

Just like your vehicle requires regular oil changes and tire rotations, your home heating and cooling system operates thousands of hours each season. Over time, dust coats the blower wheel, electrical connections loosen under vibration, and capacitors lose their microfarad tolerance.

Independent HVAC industry studies prove that over 85% of sudden AC and furnace breakdowns are directly preventable with annual maintenance. Routine tune-ups also protect your manufacturer warranty, as major brands like Carrier, Trane, and Daikin require proof of annual service to cover expensive replacement parts.

What Our 34-Point Precision Inspection Covers

We do not simply swap your filter and leave. Our NATE-certified technicians spend 60 to 90 minutes performing an exhaustive checklist:

Electrical & Controls
  • Test capacitor microfarad ratings against OEM specs
  • Inspect contactor points for pitting or burning
  • Tighten all high-voltage electrical lugs
  • Calibrate thermostat temperature sensors
  • Test safety high-limit and pressure switches
Mechanical & Airflow
  • Measure blower motor amp draw and capacitor
  • Clean flame sensor rod with fine abrasive
  • Flush condensate drain line with algaecide tabs
  • Measure temperature split (Delta-T) across coils
  • Inspect heat exchanger for cracks and CO leaks

Maintenance FAQ

How often should my heating and cooling system be serviced?

HVAC equipment should be serviced twice a year: once in the spring for your air conditioner or heat pump cooling cycle, and once in the autumn for your furnace or heating cycle.

How long does a seasonal tune-up take?

A thorough 34-point tune-up takes approximately 60 to 90 minutes. Our technician will review a full digital inspection report with you before leaving.
