Western Washington homeowners face distinct air quality challenges. During wet fall and winter months, tight building envelopes trap excess humidity, nurturing invisible mold spores and dust mites. During late summer, regional wildfire smoke routinely pushes Puget Sound Air Quality Index (AQI) ratings into hazardous zones. Standard 1-inch furnace filters only protect mechanical equipment—they do not clean the air you breathe. AeroTherma designs customized whole-home air purification systems integrated seamlessly into your existing ductwork.

Our Whole-Home Air Quality Solutions

1. Deep MERV 13 to 16 Media Cleaners

High-capacity 4-inch to 5-inch pleated media filters trap microscopic PM2.5 smoke particulates, pollen, and pet dander without suffocating your system’s airflow or raising electric bills.

2. In-Duct UV-C Germicidal Lights

Installed directly above your cooling coil, ultraviolet germicidal lamps neutralize bacteria, mold growth, and circulating viruses before they can spread through your supply vents.

4. High-Efficiency Dehumidifiers

Essential for Puget Sound basements, crawlspaces, and split-level homes. Prevents musty odors, stops wood rot, and creates an inhospitable environment for dust mites.

Frequently Asked Air Quality Questions

Can I just put a 1-inch MERV 13 filter in my furnace?

Be very cautious. Standard 1-inch filters with high MERV ratings restrict airflow dramatically because they lack sufficient surface area. This causes your blower motor to overheat and can crack your furnace heat exchanger. A dedicated 4-inch or 5-inch deep-pleated media cabinet provides 400% more surface area, achieving superior particulate capture with virtually zero airflow resistance.

Do your UV air purifiers produce hazardous ozone?

No. We exclusively install UL-2998 and CARB-certified zero-ozone germicidal air purifiers. They utilize specific 254-nanometer UV-C wavelengths that break down pathogens and volatile organic compounds without generating harmful ozone byproduct.

How often do whole-home air filter cartridges need replacement?

Unlike flimsy 1-inch filters that clog every 30 days, deep-pleated 5-inch media filters only need replacement once every 6 to 12 months under normal conditions, or after heavy summer wildfire smoke events.
