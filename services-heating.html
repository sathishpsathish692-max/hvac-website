Fast Furnace Repairs (Gas, Electric & Hybrid)

Modern furnaces are equipped with integrated electronic safety controls. When a component fails, the control board will lock out the gas valve to prevent hazards. Our NATE-certified technicians carry universal OEM replacement parts on our mobile trucks to resolve issues in a single visit:

  • Furnace Clicking 3 Times Without Lighting: Typically caused by a carbon-coated flame sensor or a cracked silicon nitride hot surface igniter (HSI).
  • Blower Fan Runs Continuously but Blows Cold Air: Often indicates a tripped high-limit switch, clogged air filter, or an open flame rollout safety fuse.
  • Draft Inducer Motor Buzzing or Whining: The inducer motor must establish a vacuum pressure draft before the gas valve will open. Worn bearings or blocked vent flues trigger pressure switch errors.
  • Yellow or Flickering Burner Flames: Natural gas should always burn a crisp, clean blue. Yellow flames indicate incomplete combustion and potential carbon monoxide risk.

Heating FAQ

How do I know if my furnace needs repair or full replacement?

If your furnace is under 12 years old and the repair cost is less than 30% of a new system, repair is usually the best choice. If the heat exchanger is cracked (a major carbon monoxide hazard) or the furnace is over 15–20 years old, upgrading to a 96%+ AFUE furnace or heat pump is far more cost-effective.

Can heat pumps really heat effectively during freezing weather?

Yes! Modern inverter-driven variable-speed heat pumps use specialized flash injection and R-410A / R-32 refrigerant cycles that extract thermal heat from outdoor air even at sub-zero temperatures. Thousands of homes across Seattle rely entirely on heat pumps for primary heating.
