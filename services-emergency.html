What Qualifies as an HVAC Emergency?

At AeroTherma, we consider any situation that threatens your family's health, safety, or home structure to be a top-priority emergency:

  • No Heat During Freezing Weather: When outdoor temperatures fall below 40°F, an unheated home poses hypothermia risks and can cause interior plumbing pipes to freeze and burst, resulting in tens of thousands in water damage.
  • Complete AC Failure During Extreme Heat: Heatwaves in modern, well-insulated homes can quickly raise indoor temperatures above 85°F, posing severe danger to infants, seniors, and pets.
  • Severe Water Leaks from Indoor Equipment: When an AC condensate line or secondary drain pan clogs, gallons of water can overflow into ceilings, drywall, and hardwood floors.
  • Electrical Burning Odors or Popping Sounds: Sparks or melting wire smells indicate high-voltage capacitor breakdown or seized blower motors that present an immediate fire hazard.
  • Suspected Carbon Monoxide or Gas Odors: If your CO detector sounds or you smell natural gas, evacuate the house immediately, call 911 or your gas utility, and call our emergency line.

Emergency Service FAQ

Do you charge outrageous overtime fees on weekends or nights?

No. We believe emergency service should be honest and transparent. We quote flat-rate diagnostic and repair prices upfront before starting any work. No surprise hourly clocks running while you sleep.

What should I do while waiting for the technician to arrive?

If your AC is leaking water, turn the thermostat to OFF immediately to stop condensation. If your furnace has shut off, check your circuit breaker once and verify the air filter isn't 100% blocked. Never attempt to handle 240V high-voltage wiring yourself.
