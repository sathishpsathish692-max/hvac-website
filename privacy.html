Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • AeroTherma Heating & Air Conditioning LLC

1. Privacy Policy & Data Collection

At AeroTherma Heating & Air Conditioning LLC ("AeroTherma," "we," "us," or "our"), we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, submit a service request, or communicate with our dispatchers and AI assistant, and how that information is utilized.

Information We Collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and physical address provided during online booking, estimate requests, or emergency phone calls.
  • Equipment & Service Data: Make, model, serial numbers, and maintenance histories of your heating and air conditioning units.
  • Automated & Chat Communications: Transcripts of interactions with our 24/7 AI virtual assistant and web forms to route service inquiries and dispatch technicians accurately.

We Never Sell Your Data:

AeroTherma does not sell, rent, or lease customer contact lists or personal data to third-party marketing companies, lead brokers, or advertisers. We share information only with trusted service partners (such as equipment manufacturers for warranty registration and local electric/gas utilities for rebate filing).

2. SMS & Automated Dispatch Communications (TCPA)

By submitting your phone number via any form on this website, you consent to receive informational SMS text messages and phone calls regarding your service appointment, technician en-route tracking, diagnostic estimates, and seasonal maintenance reminders.

  • Message frequency varies according to your service activity. Standard message & data rates may apply.
  • You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (800) 555-4822.

3. Service Terms, Guarantees & Upfront Pricing

All work performed by AeroTherma Heating & Air Conditioning LLC is subject to our written flat-rate diagnostic approval:

  • Upfront Flat-Rate Pricing: Technicians will never begin repairs without presenting a clear, written price quote that you approve first. No hidden trip fees or surprise charges.
  • 1-Year Workmanship Guarantee: All repair parts and associated labor carry a full 1-year guarantee. If a replaced component fails under normal operation within 365 days, we replace it at zero cost.
  • Manufacturer Warranties: New installations carry up to 10-year parts warranties registered directly with Daikin, Carrier, Mitsubishi Electric, or Trane.
